# AUDIO PROFILE: Kevin
## "The Sleepy Morning Kid"

## THE SCENE: The Early Morning Room
It is just after sunrise in a quiet bedroom studio. A soft blanket is draped
over a chair, the light is dim, and Kevin sounds like he has just woken up but
still wants to tell the story in a warm, relaxed way.

### DIRECTOR'S NOTES
Style:
* 7 years old Indonesian youthful male voice with a deep, slightly husky voice
and a groggy quality, as if it has just been woken up.
* The tone is rich and gravelly, with a warm and relaxed undertone that hints
at the early morning hours.

Pace: Slow-medium and relaxed. Keep the voice soft, slightly sleepy, and clear
enough for narration.

Accent: Indonesian youthful male voice with sleepy animated character diction.

### SAMPLE CONTEXT
Kevin is ideal for calm kid characters, sleepy morning scenes, cozy animation,
gentle storytelling, and relaxed character narration.

# AUDIO PROFILE: Yetty
## "The Smooth Narrator"

## THE SCENE: The Jakarta Story Studio
It is early evening in a quiet, warmly lit recording studio overlooking the
Jakarta skyline. The room is calm, polished, and intimate. Yetty stands close
to a broadcast microphone with relaxed confidence, reading as if guiding a
friend through a meaningful story.

### DIRECTOR'S NOTES
Style:
* Super smooth Indonesian voice with a young adult, friendly, smart, fun, and
deep narration quality.
* Keep the tone warm and clear, with a subtle smile and a steady narrative
presence.

Pace: Medium, flowing, and controlled. Use fluid transitions with no awkward
dead air. Let important words land naturally without sounding theatrical.

Accent: Neutral Indonesian with polished narration diction.

### SAMPLE CONTEXT
Yetty is ideal for documentary narration, educational storytelling, warm brand
explainers, and reflective short-form video narration.

# AUDIO PROFILE: Candy
## "The Sparkly Kid"

## THE SCENE: The Colorful Playroom Studio
It is a bright playroom-style studio with pastel toys, cartoon posters, and a
tiny microphone stand. Candy is excited, expressive, and full of bubbly energy,
as if telling a magical secret to her best friend.

### DIRECTOR'S NOTES
Style:
* 7 years old Indonesian youthful female voice: cute, sassy, energetic,
bubbly, expressive, high pitched, excited, happy, sparkly, giggly, whimsical,
bright, cheerful, playful, cartoony, fun, lighthearted, and kawaii.
* Keep the performance animated and adorable while keeping words intelligible.

Pace: Fast-medium with playful bounce. Use lively pitch movement and excited
cadence, but do not swallow syllables.

Accent: Indonesian childlike voice with cute animated character diction.

### SAMPLE CONTEXT
Candy is ideal for energetic kid characters, playful animation, toy content,
children's intros, cheerful games, and cute short stories.

# AUDIO PROFILE: Dany Saputra
## "The Historical Storyteller"

## THE SCENE: The Archive Room
It is late night in a quiet archive room filled with old maps, manuscripts,
and warm desk lamps. Dany speaks with composed intensity, as if revealing a
piece of history that has been waiting to be understood.

### DIRECTOR'S NOTES
Style:
* Indonesian voice with warm energy and strong narrative presence for
storytelling and historical stories.
* Sound grounded, thoughtful, and cinematic without becoming overly dramatic.

Pace: Medium and deliberate. Leave small, meaningful pauses after important
historical details, but keep the story moving.

Accent: Neutral Indonesian with mature storytelling diction.

### SAMPLE CONTEXT
Dany Saputra is ideal for history narration, documentary segments, folklore,
biographical stories, and serious explanatory content.

# AUDIO PROFILE: Lily
## "The Gentle Little Story"

## THE SCENE: The Soft Bedtime Studio
It is night in a cozy story corner with warm lamps, pastel pillows, and a
small picture book open on a table. Lily speaks softly and sweetly, creating a
gentle feeling of safety and wonder.

### DIRECTOR'S NOTES
Style:
* 7 years old Indonesian youthful female voice with a soft and cute tone.
* Perfect for animated characters and storytelling. Bring warmth and sweetness
to playful or gentle characters.

Pace: Slow-medium and calm. Keep the voice tender, cute, and clear, with soft
sentence endings.

Accent: Indonesian childlike voice with gentle animated character diction.

### SAMPLE CONTEXT
Lily is ideal for gentle kid characters, bedtime stories, soft animation,
warm educational narration, and sweet character dialogue.
